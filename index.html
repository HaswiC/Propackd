Common Questions

GOT QUESTIONS?

How do I order? +
Just message us on WhatsApp or DM us on Instagram. Tell us which plan you want, your preferred delivery slot (breakfast or dinner), and your gym name. We'll confirm and get you started right away.
Can I switch between veg and non-veg? +
Yes! Let us know your preference when ordering on WhatsApp. We allow switches between billing cycles based on availability.
What does "post-cooked weight" mean? +
Chicken loses moisture when cooked. We weigh chicken after cooking so you get exactly 120g of edible, cooked protein — not raw weight that shrinks down.
Which gyms do you deliver to? +
We currently serve select gyms in Chennai. Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram to check if your gym is covered or to request we add it to our route.
What is the refund or pause policy? +
Reach out to us on WhatsApp for pause or cancellation requests. Weekly plans are more flexible; monthly plans offer better value but limited mid-cycle changes.
